Security & Privacy

Protect Your Account & Information

Your account, personal information and work activity are important. Every member is responsible for protecting their own account and using PRO EARN carefully and honestly.

🔐 Account Security
Protect your PRO EARN account and keep your login information private.
Use a strong password that is difficult for other people to guess.
Never share your password with other people.
Do not publicly post your password or sensitive account information.
Avoid logging into your account on public or unknown devices.
Log out after using another device.
Never allow another person to use your account.
Do not give another person permission to access your account.
Regularly check your account activity and dashboard.
If you believe someone accessed your account, change your password and contact support.
Official support should not require you to publicly disclose your password.
👤 Members' Information & Privacy
Treat other members' personal information with respect.
Do not share another member's name, email, phone or payment information without permission.
Do not collect unnecessary personal information.
Do not copy, publish or misuse another member's private information.
Do not post members' information in public groups or social media.
Use information only for an authorized purpose.
Do not attempt to access another member's account.
Respect the privacy of every member.
Keep your own account information accurate.
🤝 Honest & Responsible Work
Complete available work honestly.
Follow the instructions provided for each assignment.
Do not submit false or intentionally incorrect information.
Do not submit another member's work as your own.
Do not use another person's account or identity.
Do not manipulate the work system to receive rewards.
Keep your work activity genuine.
All assignments must be handwritten on A4-size paper in English or Urdu.
Copy-paste or AI-generated assignments are not accepted.
Submitting copied or AI-generated work may lead to account review and could result in permanent account suspension.
If an assignment is unclear, contact official support.
⚠️ Rules & Violations
Follow applicable PRO EARN rules and instructions.
Do not bypass platform restrictions or security controls.
Do not attempt unauthorized access to Firebase data or another account.
Do not create multiple accounts to manipulate rewards or referrals.
Do not provide false information.
Do not use another person's identity.
Do not misuse referral links.
Do not manipulate referral activity.
Do not bypass approval or withdrawal procedures.
Do not interfere with another member's account or rewards.
Report security issues to official support.
Violations may result in review, restriction or suspension where appropriate.
🔗 Referral & Affiliate Safety
Use your referral link responsibly.
Provide truthful information about the referral program.
Do not pressure or deceive people.
Do not use spam or suspicious websites.
Do not create accounts through your own referral link.
Do not manipulate referral numbers.
Referral activity should represent genuine members.
💳 Payment & Withdrawal Safety
Check your payment account number and withdrawal amount carefully.
Use your own correct payment information.
Do not share payment details publicly.
Do not intentionally submit incorrect payment information.
There may be one pending withdrawal request at a time.
Wait for an existing withdrawal request when required.
Review the withdrawal amount before submitting.
📱 Safe Platform Usage
Keep your device protected with a screen lock or password.
Keep your browser and device updated.
Avoid suspicious links claiming to be PRO EARN links.
Do not enter your PRO EARN password into unknown websites.
Use official PRO EARN pages and support channels.
Check the website address before entering sensitive information.
Do not provide passwords to suspicious messages.
📋 Account Responsibility
Every member is responsible for protecting their login credentials.
Every member is responsible for work submitted through their account.
Keep account information accurate and updated.
Review instructions before completing work.
Check your dashboard for work, plans, wallet and withdrawal updates.
If you no longer trust a device, log out and secure your account.
IMPORTANT:

Protect your account, keep your personal information private, work honestly and follow the applicable PRO EARN rules.

If you notice suspicious activity, contact the official PRO EARN support team instead of sharing sensitive information publicly.
