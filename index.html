Security & Privacy

Protect Your Account & Information

Your account, personal information and work activity are important. Every member is responsible for protecting their own account and using PRO EARN carefully and honestly. Please read the following security, privacy and responsible-use guidelines before using the platform.

🔐 Account Security
Protect your PRO EARN account and keep your login information private at all times.
Use a strong password that is difficult for other people to guess.
Never share your password with friends, family members, unknown persons or anyone claiming to need it.
Do not publicly post your password, email password, verification information or other sensitive account details.
Use your account on your own trusted mobile device whenever possible.
Avoid logging into your account on public, shared or unknown devices.
If you use another device temporarily, make sure you log out after finishing your session.
Never allow another person to use your PRO EARN account as if it were their own account.
Do not give another person permission to access your account, even if they claim they can help you with your work.
Regularly check your account activity and dashboard so you can notice anything unusual.
If you believe someone has accessed your account without permission, change your password and contact official support as soon as possible.
PRO EARN support should not require you to publicly disclose your password. Keep sensitive login information private.
👤 Members' Information & Privacy
Treat other members' personal information with respect and keep it private.
Do not share another member's name, email address, phone number, payment information or account details without permission.
Do not collect unnecessary personal information from other members.
Do not copy, publish, misuse or distribute another member's private account information.
Do not post members' personal or payment information in public groups, social media pages or other public places.
Use only the information that is genuinely required for an authorized work or communication purpose.
Do not attempt to access another member's account, password, wallet, withdrawal information or private activity.
Respect the privacy of every member and do not use another person's information for an unauthorized purpose.
Keep your own personal information accurate and only provide information that is required by the platform.
🤝 Honest & Responsible Work
Complete your available work honestly and responsibly.
Follow the instructions provided for each assignment and work category.
Do not submit false, misleading or intentionally incorrect information just to claim a reward.
Do not copy and paste assignments simply to receive a reward. Complete the assignment according to the instructions provided.
Do not submit another member's work as your own.
Do not use another person's account or identity to complete work or claim rewards.
Do not intentionally manipulate the work system or attempt to receive rewards for work that was not genuinely completed.
Use the platform in a responsible manner and keep your work activity genuine.
If an assignment is unclear, review the instructions carefully or contact official support instead of submitting random information.
Honest work helps maintain a fair and organized environment for all members.
⚠️ Rules & Violations
Follow the applicable PRO EARN rules and instructions when using your account.
Do not intentionally bypass platform restrictions, security controls or account limits.
Do not attempt unauthorized access to the website, Firebase data, another member's account or any private system information.
Do not create or use multiple accounts for the purpose of manipulating rewards, referrals, work records or other platform activity.
Do not provide false information when creating or using an account.
Do not use another person's identity or account information.
Do not misuse referral links or attempt to manipulate the referral system.
Do not create an account through your own referral link or use prohibited referral arrangements involving your own accounts.
Do not promote your referral link to family members where such activity is prohibited by the platform rules.
Do not use misleading claims or false information when sharing your referral link with other people.
Do not attempt to bypass approval, withdrawal or work-related procedures.
Do not interfere with another member's account, work, rewards or withdrawal activity.
If a security issue or unusual activity is discovered, report it to official support instead of attempting to misuse it.
Violation of applicable rules may result in review, restriction, suspension or other action on the account where appropriate.
🔗 Referral & Affiliate Safety
Use your referral link responsibly and provide clear and truthful information when sharing it.
Do not pressure, mislead or deceive people when explaining the referral program.
Do not use spam, suspicious websites or unsafe sources to distribute your referral link.
Do not promote your referral link to family members where the platform rules prohibit such activity.
Do not create accounts yourself through your own referral link.
Do not attempt to manipulate the number of referrals or create artificial referral activity.
Referral activity should represent genuine members using their own accounts.
💳 Payment & Withdrawal Safety
Check your payment method, account number, account holder name and withdrawal amount carefully before submitting a withdrawal request.
Use your own correct payment information when submitting a withdrawal request.
Do not share your payment account details publicly or with unknown persons.
Use the same withdrawal account or bank/payment details consistently once you have used them, according to the applicable platform rules.
Do not submit incorrect payment information intentionally.
You may have one pending withdrawal request at a time according to the current platform rules.
Wait for the existing withdrawal request to be completed before submitting another request when the platform requires this.
Review the withdrawal amount carefully before submitting the request.
📱 Safe Platform Usage
Keep your device protected with a screen lock, password or other suitable security method.
Keep your browser and mobile device updated whenever updates are available.
Avoid opening suspicious links that claim to be related to your PRO EARN account.
Do not enter your PRO EARN password into unknown websites or forms.
Use official PRO EARN pages and official support channels when dealing with account-related matters.
Check the website address before entering sensitive account information.
If you receive a suspicious message asking for your password or account access, do not provide the information and contact official support.
📋 Account Responsibility
Every member is responsible for protecting their own login credentials and account information.
Every member is responsible for the work submitted through their own account.
Every member should keep their account information accurate and up to date.
Members should review the available instructions before completing work or submitting requests.
Members should check their dashboard regularly for updates to their work, plans, wallet and withdrawal status.
If you no longer trust a device where your account was used, log out and secure your account before continuing.
IMPORTANT:

Protect your account, keep your personal information private, work honestly and follow the applicable PRO EARN rules. Do not attempt to violate, bypass or misuse the platform's rules, security controls or work procedures.

If you notice suspicious activity, an account-security problem or a privacy concern, contact the official PRO EARN support team instead of sharing sensitive information publicly.

Using PRO EARN means you should use the platform responsibly, protect your account and respect the privacy and information of other members.
